// About ZAHA Labs

We're a small independent studio building digital products the right way — no dark patterns, no hype, no locked-in subscriptions for basic features. Every property gets the same treatment: clear purpose, honest affiliate disclosure, educational content and tools that work offline-first where possible.

Founded on the principle that useful software and honest content can still be a sustainable business — and that users deserve to know exactly how a site earns money.

  • Tools — free utilities that genuinely help
  • Content — researched guides, no AI slop
  • Products — books, printables & extensions
  • Affiliates — recommended only when they earn it
  • Privacy — client-side where possible, no tracking abuse
